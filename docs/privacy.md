# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026.

This policy describes how **DNS** (`https://dns.thaler.finance`) handles information. DNS (Delta Neutral Stocks) is a Thaler product for delta-neutral stock exposure on Solana.

Contact: use the channels listed on [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance) or open an issue with the project maintainers if a public repository is linked from the site.

## Summary

- DNS is a trading / finance interface on Solana. On-chain balances, positions, and transactions you authorize are public on Solana.
- Wallet connection uses Solana-compatible wallets.
- DNS does not sell your personal data.
- DNS does not load analytics or advertising pixels unless you allow analytics cookies.

## What is public on Solana

| Piece | Where it lives | Who can read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet address | Solana | Anyone with an explorer or RPC |
| Signed transactions | Solana | Anyone |
| Token balances and program accounts you interact with | Solana | Anyone who can query the relevant accounts |

Do not treat a blockchain address as private. Anyone who knows your address can see related on-chain activity.

## What we collect

**Account**

- Connected Solana wallet address.

**Product use**

- Actions you take in the app (for example connecting, signing, or submitting transactions) as needed to operate the service.
- Optional agent-signer preference for your session (see [Cookie Policy](/cookies)).

**Technical**

- Server logs that your host may keep (IP, user agent, request path) to operate the site.
- Necessary cookies and storage for theme and preferences. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

We do not ask for payment card data through DNS. Network fees (SOL and any protocol fees) are paid on Solana to those networks or programs, not as a card charge to DNS.

## External services

Solana RPC providers and any trading / oracle / infrastructure partners used by the app see the public requests and transactions required to run the product.

## Cookies and tracking

Necessary cookies and storage run basic preferences. Analytics and advertising do not run unless you allow them in the cookie banner. Details: [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where GDPR applies: contract (to run the account and service you request), legitimate interests (to operate and secure the site), and consent (optional analytics). You may object or withdraw consent as described in the Cookie Policy and below.

## Retention

On-chain data remains on Solana according to that network. Server logs follow the host’s retention. Consent choice stays in your browser until you clear it or change it.

## Your rights

You may disconnect your wallet. You may change or clear cookie consent as described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

You cannot erase public Solana history with a button in DNS.

## Children

DNS is not for children under 18. Do not create an account for a minor.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. Continued use after a change means you accept the new text.
