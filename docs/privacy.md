# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

This policy describes how **DNS** (`https://dns.thaler.finance`) handles information. DNS (Delta Neutral Stocks) is a Thaler product for delta-neutral stock exposure on Solana. "We", "us", and "our" mean the operators of DNS, who act as data controller for the processing described below.

Contact: use the channels listed on [thaler.finance](https://thaler.finance) or open an issue with the project maintainers if a public repository is linked from the site.

## Summary

- DNS is a trading / finance interface on Solana. On-chain balances, positions, and transactions you authorize are public on Solana.
- Wallet connection uses Solana-compatible wallets.
- DNS does not sell your personal data.
- DNS does not load analytics or advertising pixels unless you allow analytics cookies.
- DNS is not the controller for data processed by independent tokenized-equity issuers (e.g. xStocks) or by third-party wallets and RPC providers — those parties have their own privacy notices.

## What is public on Solana

| Piece | Where it lives | Who can read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet address | Solana | Anyone with an explorer or RPC |
| Signed transactions | Solana | Anyone |
| Token balances and program accounts you interact with | Solana | Anyone who can query the relevant accounts |

Do not treat a blockchain address as private. Anyone who knows your address can see related on-chain activity.

## What we collect, how we use it, and our legal basis

**Account**

- Connected Solana wallet address.
- *Use:* to operate your session and let you interact with DNS. *Legal basis:* performance of a contract with you (providing the service you request).

**Product use**

- Actions you take in the app (for example connecting, signing, or submitting transactions) as needed to operate the service.
- Optional agent-signer preference for your session (see [Cookie Policy](/cookies)).
- *Use:* to run the requested feature, prevent fraud, and secure the service. *Legal basis:* performance of a contract, and our legitimate interest in keeping DNS secure and working.

**Technical**

- Server logs that your host may keep (IP, user agent, request path) to operate the site.
- Necessary cookies and storage for theme and preferences. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
- Optional analytics data, only if you opt in via the cookie banner.
- *Use:* operate, secure, and improve the site. *Legal basis:* legitimate interest (necessary operation and security) or your consent (optional analytics).

We do not ask for payment card data through DNS. Network fees (SOL and any protocol fees) are paid on Solana to those networks or programs, not as a card charge to DNS.

## Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

- **Service providers** who host, secure, or operate infrastructure for DNS, under confidentiality obligations and solely to provide the service.
- **Solana RPC providers and infrastructure/trading partners** who see the public requests and transactions required to run the product.
- **Tokenized-equity issuers** (e.g. Backed Finance AG for xStocks) only to the extent required by the transactions you initiate; they act as an independent controller for their own processing under their own privacy notice.
- **Law enforcement or regulators** where required by law, subpoena, or court order, or where we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of a third party.
- **A successor** in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this policy continuing to apply to your data.

## International transfers

Where we or our service providers process data outside your home country, we take steps to ensure an equivalent level of protection, such as relying on adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or equivalent safeguards required by applicable law. Contact us if you would like more detail on the safeguards used for a specific transfer.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the data involved, including encryption in transit and access controls limiting internal access to a need-to-know basis. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protecting your wallet keys and device is your responsibility.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including GDPR for EEA/UK/Swiss individuals), you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten"), where it is no longer needed, consent is withdrawn, or processing was unlawful.
- **Restrict** processing in certain circumstances.
- **Port** data you provided to us, in a structured, machine-readable format, where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means.
- **Object** to processing based on our legitimate interests.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for processing based on consent (e.g. optional analytics), without affecting prior lawful processing.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact channels above. We may ask for reasonable proof of identity first. We cannot alter or erase data that is public on the Solana blockchain — see above.

## Retention

On-chain data remains on Solana according to that network and is outside our control. Server logs follow the host's retention schedule. Your cookie-consent choice stays in your browser until you clear it or change it. We retain other personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

## Cookies and tracking

Necessary cookies and storage run basic preferences. Analytics and advertising do not run unless you allow them in the cookie banner. Details: [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## Automated decisions
