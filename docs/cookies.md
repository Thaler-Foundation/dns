# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026.

This page describes cookies and similar storage on **DNS** (`https://dns.thaler.finance`). Related: [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer or the banner.

## How we use storage

DNS uses cookies and web storage for three jobs:

1. **Necessary:** sign-in and security. These run so the site can work.
2. **Preferences:** your cookie choice and theme preference.
3. **Analytics:** measurement. These do **not** load unless you allow analytics.

DNS does not use advertising cookies today. If that changes, they will stay off until you allow them.

## Necessary (always on)

| Name / storage | Who | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privy session cookies and local storage | Privy | Email or Google sign-in, embedded Solana wallet |
| `dns-agent:` session flags | DNS | Remember that you enabled the agent signer in this tab |
| Theme preference (`next-themes` / class on `html`) | DNS | Light or dark appearance |

Without necessary storage, you cannot stay signed in. The cookie banner does not block these.

If you use **Google** login, Google may set its own cookies on Google’s domains. That is Google’s control. Read [Google cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## Preferences

| Name / storage | Who | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dns-consent-v1` in `localStorage` | DNS | Stores whether you allowed analytics |

## Analytics (off until you allow)

DNS does not load Google Analytics, ads pixels, or similar tags unless your stored choice has `analytics: true`.

If you pick **Necessary only**, no analytics tag runs.

If you pick **Allow analytics**, DNS may measure page views with a privacy-respecting tag. We will name that tag here when one is wired. Until then, Allow analytics stores your consent and still loads no extra tag.

## How to control cookies

- Use the banner: **Necessary only** or **Allow analytics**.
- Use **Cookie settings** in the footer.
- Clear site data in your browser to wipe Privy session and `dns-consent-v1`.

Browser “Do Not Track” does not change necessary sign-in cookies.
